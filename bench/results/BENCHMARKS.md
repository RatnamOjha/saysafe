## Voice ID on LibriSpeech

LibriSpeech test-clean (CC BY 4.0), 40 speakers, 3 enrollment + 8 test utterances each, seed 7. speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb + Silero VAD, CPU on Apple M3.

Every cut of speech scored against every enrolled speaker, at the packaged thresholds:

| Speech | Owner accepted | Stranger accepted | EER | No score | Score time (p50) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 s | 68 of 320 (21.2%) | 0 of 12480 (0%) | 5.9% | 0 of 12800 (0%) | 10.9 ms |
| 1.0 s | 235 of 320 (73.4%) | 1 of 12480 (0.008%) | 1.6% | 40 of 12800 (0.3%) | 16.1 ms |
| 2.0 s | 308 of 320 (96.2%) | 1 of 12480 (0.008%) | 0.6% | 0 of 12800 (0%) | 26.0 ms |
| 3.0 s | 315 of 320 (98.4%) | 2 of 12480 (0.016%) | 0.4% | 0 of 12800 (0%) | 36.0 ms |

End to end through `ApprovalGuard` (20 other speakers, voice tier, reply 'yes', command 3.0 s, both from the same person). Calibrated thresholds (accept 0.293, reject 0.201) come from `saysafe.calibrate` on 20 dev speakers, 1.0 s cuts (159 owner, 3021 stranger scores).

| Thresholds, reply length | Owner approved by voice | Stranger approved |
|---|---|---|
| packaged, reply 0.5 s | 126 of 160 (78.8%) | 0 of 760 (0%) |
| packaged, reply 1.0 s | 151 of 160 (94.4%) | 0 of 760 (0%) |
| calibrated, reply 0.5 s | 147 of 160 (91.9%) | 0 of 760 (0%) |
| calibrated, reply 1.0 s | 159 of 160 (99.4%) | 2 of 760 (0.3%) |

An owner who isn't approved by voice isn't refused: the step goes to a phone tap. A stranger who is approved has passed the voice tier, which the default policy keeps for purchases under $75 and emails to known contacts.

LibriSpeech is clean read speech, close to the best case. A wearable mic in a noisy room will do worse, and recordings or voice clones of the owner aren't tested here: calibrate on your own recordings, and read the threat model.
